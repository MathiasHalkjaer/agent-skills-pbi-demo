# Glossary

The business words for what's in the model, and the words people use for them. Use the
first column's word in names and descriptions.

| Business term | Words people also use | In the model |
|---|---|---|
| Sales | revenue, turnover, invoiced | Total Sales: all invoiced lines, returns included |
| Net Sales | net revenue, headline sales | Net Sales: excludes returned lines |
| Cost | COGS, cost of goods | Total Cost |
| Margin | gross margin, GM | Margin %: (sales minus cost) divided by sales |
| Segment | customer type | Customer[Segment]: Enterprise, Mid-market, Small business |
| Return | credit, credited line | A sales line with Is Return = 1 |
